# Art Style (locked)

- Flat cartoon, top-down 3/4 view, drawn as SVG
- 3px outline in warm brown (#3b2a20), never pure black
- Flat fills only, with no gradients or glossy highlights
- Rounded corners, and one soft oval shadow under anything standing on the ground
- Quiet dusty ground, with the bright colors saved for cloth, awnings, umbrellas and signs

Palette: outline #3b2a20, dirt #d8b98a / #c49a64, wood #8d5524, orange #f39c12,
teal #1fa7a0, magenta #d6336c, yellow #f6c026, blue #2f5fd0, leaf #3a7d44,
cream #fff4dc, sachet blue #8fd0f5

People: men and women in three age groups (teen, adult, elder). Hair, caps and
headwraps are separate layers. The player always wears an orange cap.

Ownership: all art is original to this project, drawn as code with AI assistance.
