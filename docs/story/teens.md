# Chapter 2: The teenage years (ages 13 to 19)

About four hours of play: around eleven **big days**, with shorter time jumps than childhood (life moves fast
at this age). This is where childhood's secrets pay off: the father, the uncle, Nico's drift, Gran's health,
Mr Ambrose's shop, Juno, Mrs Pell's son. Everything here depends on what you did as a child.

### 1. Age 13: Two schools
- If you won the scholarship: your first day at **Urlivaven Central**, across the city. New uniform, rich
  kids, a long bus ride, Juno as the only face you know. Nico waves you off from the lane, then stops waiting.
- If you didn't, or went to Nico's final: **Sunhill Secondary** with Nico, rougher and louder. Juno is gone,
  and you hear about her life at Central second-hand.
- **Hook:** Corin is waiting at the school gate. "Ten minutes. That's all I'm asking."

### 2. Age 13: Ten minutes with Corin
- In a café in Old Harbour, Corin tells you your father is alive, "somewhere he can't leave yet", and wants
  to see you. He asks you not to tell your mother.
- **Choice:** agree to keep it secret, tell Mum that night, or walk out. Each sets how the next years go
  (`met-corin`, `secret-from-mum`).
- **Hook:** Corin asks to borrow "just twenty livs, till Friday".

### 3. Age 14: The first hustle
- DailyVa is crushing Mr Ambrose's shop. You have an idea to save it: deliveries by bike to the old people on
  the hill, a noticeboard, a "Still Here" loyalty card. Or Nico has an idea of his own: reselling DailyVa
  stock out of the back of his cousin's van, no questions asked.
- **Choice:** Mr Ambrose's way, Nico's way, or your own small business (mending like Gran, repairing phones,
  baking). Your first real money, and your first real business decisions (`first-hustle`).
- **Hook:** it works. Too well. Somebody notices.

### 4. Age 14: The Rows
- Nico takes you to a party in The Rows. His new friends are older, funny, and dangerous. Later that night,
  someone breaks the window of the DailyVa on the high street.
- **Choice:** leave early, stay, or try to drag Nico home. If you stayed, the police knock on Nico's door in
  the morning, and maybe yours (`rows-night`).
- **Hook:** Nico asks you to say he was with you all night.

### 5. Age 15: The truth
- Mum finds out (about Corin, the letters, the money you lent him, or all of it). The biggest fight of your
  life in the kitchen, with Gran caught in the middle and your little sibling listening from the stairs.
- The truth comes out: your father took the blame for something Corin did, and went to prison. He's due out
  this year. Mum never forgave him for choosing his brother over his family, and never gave you the letters.
- **Choice:** take Mum's side, Gran's side, your father's side, or walk out and stay at Nico's or Mrs Pell's.
  This shapes your relationship with Mum for the rest of her life (`the-truth`).
- **Hook:** "Your father gets out on Friday. Will you be there?"

### 6. Age 15: The gate
- The prison gate at dawn. A thin, older man walks out and stops when he sees you. He laughs, and you realise
  you laugh exactly like him.
- **Choice:** hug him, shake his hand, or turn around and leave. Whatever you choose, Corin is there too, and
  your father won't look at him (`met-father`).
- **Hook:** your father says, "There's something else your mother didn't tell you."

### 7. Age 16: First love
- Someone special: maybe Juno (rivals for years, until one night it isn't rivalry), maybe someone new from
  school or the lane. A first date at the Kestrel Park fair; first heartbreak or not. Family-friendly and
  sweet, with real stakes for your heart (`first-love`).
- **Hook:** you see Juno crying alone at the bus stop at midnight.

### 8. Age 16: Juno's door
- Juno's parents are splitting up and the house is a war zone. She turns up at your door at night with a bag.
- **Choice:** let her stay (Mum is not pleased, Gran is), call her parents, or turn her away. It decides
  whether she becomes your closest ally, a lifelong debt, or a bitter enemy (`juno-stayed`).
- **Hook:** Juno's father comes looking for her, and he knows exactly who your father is.

### 9. Age 17: Gran
- Gran's health fails. A last good afternoon on the sofa, mending, singing, Biscuit at her feet. If you're close,
  she tells you the last piece of the truth, and gives you the song her mother sang to her.
- Her funeral: the whole lane comes. Mum and your father in the same room for the first time in fifteen years.
  Who you stand beside matters (`gran-died`).
- **Hook:** among Gran's things, an envelope with your name on it, and a key.

### 10. Age 18: What now?
- Final exams, and then the big choice of the teens: university (if your grades allow), a job, Mr Ambrose
  offering you a share of the shop, your father asking you to help him start over, or Corin's "simple job"
  with very good money. Mrs Pell, now frail, asks you to find her son Daniel before it's too late.
- **Choice:** what you do with your life next, which opens the young adult chapter (`path-after-school`).
- **Hook:** the key from Gran's envelope opens a safety deposit box at First Urlivana Bank.

### 11. Age 19: Leaving home
- Your little sibling (now 12) is either following in your footsteps or furious with you. Nico is in trouble,
  out of trouble, or out of your life. You pack a bag.
- **Choice:** leave home for your own place, stay to help Mum, or move in with your father.
- **Hook (end of the teens):** what's in Gran's box changes everything: a deed to a small, forgotten shop in
  Old Harbour, in your name.

---

### Everyday life between the big days
School, homework and exams; part-time work and your hustle; friends and phones; Nico's schemes; Gran's mending
and Biscuit growing old; your sibling growing up; the lane changing around you as DailyVa takes over; money of
your own for the first time, and what you choose to do with it.

### What carries into adulthood
Your relationship with Mum, your father, Corin and your sibling; Nico's fate; Juno; the money and skills you
built; what you studied or where you worked; Mrs Pell's son; and Gran's shop in Old Harbour.
