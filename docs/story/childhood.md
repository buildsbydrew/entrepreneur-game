# Chapter 1: Childhood (ages 5 to 12)

About two hours of play: nine **big days** that are always played, with time jumps between them. Each big day
has one or two choices that matter, consequences that show up later, and a **hook** to end on (these become
the cliffhangers of the AI life videos). Everyday conversations fill the days around them.

Cast: see `cast.md`. Flags named here (`like-this`) are what the game remembers.

---

### 1. Age 5: The first day out alone *(built)*
- Mum lets you out on Willow Lane by yourself for the first time. Nico, the park, Mrs Pell's flowers,
  Mr Ambrose's sweets with Gran's 3 livs.
- **New:** at dusk, you notice a man in a green coat across the lane, watching your house. When you look
  again, he's gone. If you tell Mum, she goes very quiet and checks the lock twice (`saw-green-coat`).
- **Hook:** "That night, you heard Mum and Gran arguing in whispers. You only caught one word: *Corin*."

### 2. Age 5: The first day at Sunhill Primary
- New place: Sunhill Primary (classroom, playground, gate). Mr Hale's class. Juno Calder sits next to you,
  corrects your spelling, and wins the class's first gold star.
- **Choice:** when Juno gets the answer you were about to give: stay quiet, say "I knew that too", or tease her
  at playtime. Sets the tone of the rivalry for years (`juno-rivalry`: friendly, bitter or cruel).
- **Hook:** Mr Hale writes something in a notebook while watching you, and closes it when you look.

### 3. Age 6: The tab
- Sent to the corner shop with Mum's note, you overhear Mr Ambrose on the phone: "The Varells owe almost two
  hundred now. I can't keep doing it." He sees you, smiles, and gives you a free sweet anyway.
- **Choice:** tell Gran, tell Mum, or keep it to yourself. Telling Gran starts a fight between Gran and Mum;
  telling Mum makes her cry and swear you to secrecy; keeping quiet means you carry it alone
  (`knows-about-tab`, `told-gran` / `told-mum`).
- **Hook:** that week, Mum's dinner plate is always half empty. She says she isn't hungry.

### 4. Age 7: The baby
- Mum goes into hospital to have the baby. You and Gran wait at home through a long night. You name your little
  brother or sister.
- Midway through the night, the man in the green coat knocks with a wrapped gift. Gran shouts him off the step.
  **Choice:** open the gift before Gran sees, give it to Gran, or throw it away. The gift is a carved wooden bird
  and a note: *"For the new one. Your father would want you to know he thinks of you. C."*
  (`has-bird`, `read-note`).
- **Hook:** Gran, looking at the note, says quietly to no one: "He has no right."

### 5. Age 8: Mrs Pell's birthday
- It's Mrs Pell's birthday. She's put out cake and good cups for her son Daniel. Nobody comes.
- **Choice:** go and sit with her, make her a card with your little sibling, or go to play with Nico instead.
  Sitting with her, she shows you a photo of a boy on a bike and almost tells you why he never visits
  (`pell-birthday`). It counts for a lot, years from now.
- **Hook:** after you leave, you see her through the window, putting the second cup back in the cupboard.

### 6. Age 9: Nico takes something
- Nico's dad has lost his job. In Mr Ambrose's shop, you see Nico slip bread and a bag of rice into his coat.
  Mr Ambrose turns around and asks you directly: "Did you see anything?"
- **Choice:** lie for Nico, tell the truth, or say nothing and leave. Each one changes Nico, Mr Ambrose, and
  what you think of yourself (`covered-for-nico` / `told-on-nico`). If you lie, Nico owes you, and remembers.
  If you tell, Mr Ambrose quietly lets the Brasks have a tab too.
- **Hook:** "Nico didn't speak to you for a week. When he did, it was different."

### 7. Age 10: The box
- Looking for a blanket, you find a shoebox at the top of Mum's wardrobe, full of letters addressed to you.
  The same handwriting, one every year, all unopened.
- **Choice:** read one, put the box back and say nothing, or take it to Gran. Reading one tells you your father
  is alive and "away" (not where); taking it to Gran makes her say "It's not my secret to tell. Yet."
  (`found-letters`, `read-letter`).
- **Hook (end of a video episode):** Mum is standing in the doorway, watching you hold the box.

### 8. Age 11: The scholarship
- Mr Hale puts you and Juno forward for the scholarship exam to Urlivaven Central. It's the same morning as
  Nico's football final, which he's begged you to come to.
- **Choice:** sit the exam, or go to Nico's final. If you sit it, a small skill-and-choice challenge decides
  how you do; Juno is in the seat next to you, and her mother doesn't come to collect her
  (`took-exam`, `won-scholarship`, `nico-final`).
- **Hook:** two letters arrive the same day: one from Urlivaven Central, one in your father's handwriting.

### 9. Age 12: Two celebrations, and a fall
- Mum's nursing graduation is the same evening that DailyVa, the new supermarket, opens with a free party, and
  Mr Ambrose asks you to help hand out leaflets for his shop's "Still Here" sale.
- **Choice:** go to Mum's graduation (she looks for you in the crowd), help Mr Ambrose, or go to the free party
  with Nico. Each one bends a relationship that matters in the teens.
- **The fall:** that night, Gran collapses in the kitchen. She's okay, this time. In the hospital corridor,
  you see the man in the green coat, who says your name.
- **Hook (end of childhood):** "Corin Varell. I'm your uncle. We need to talk about your father."

---

### Everyday life between the big days
- **Home:** helping Gran with mending, Biscuit the dog, meals at the table, Mum asleep on the sofa after
  shifts, chores for a few livs, homework, your little sibling following you around.
- **The lane:** Nico's schemes, Mrs Pell's stories and flowers, buying sweets, a ball in the park.
- **School:** Mr Hale's lessons, Juno's rivalry, playground games, the walk home.
- **Small money moments:** saving livs for something you want, a first small way to earn (sweeping, errands,
  selling Gran's mended clothes), noticing prices.

### What carries into the teens
Which secrets you know, how close you are to Mum and Gran, whether Nico owes you or resents you, what Juno
thinks of you, whether you went to Central or stayed with Nico, Mrs Pell's trust, Mr Ambrose's shop, and
what you've decided to do about your father.
