# Cast

The people of your childhood in Urlivaven. Everyone has a life of their own, a secret or a wound, and a
storyline that keeps paying off for decades. They remember how you treated them.

The rule for drama: **every secret is planted in childhood, felt in the teens, and pays off as an adult.**
Nothing is good or evil; everyone has a reason.

Setting: **Sunhill**, an older, quieter district of Urlivaven where families live, and **Willow Lane**, the
street you grow up on. The money is **livs**.

---

## The Varell family

### Lena Varell (Mum), 32 when you're 5
- Cleans at Urlivaven City Hospital on long shifts and studies to be a nurse at night. Proud, tired, funny
  when she has the energy. Loves you fiercely, shows it badly when she's exhausted.
- **Secret:** she knows why your father left, and keeps his letters in a box at the top of her wardrobe. She
  also owes Mr Ambrose a growing amount for groceries on credit.
- **Wound:** she gave up her own plans when she had you, and sometimes it shows.
- **Arc:** qualifies as a nurse when you're 12 (if things go right). In your teens, the truth about your father
  comes out, and how you react decides whether you stay close. Later, she needs you as she ages.

### Ilsa Varell (Gran), 71 when you're 5
- Mends clothes for the neighbourhood from the front room, which quietly pays a lot of the bills. Sings old
  songs, has opinions about everything, clashes with Lena about how you're raised. Has an old dog, **Biscuit**.
- **Secret:** she knows the truth about your father too, and thinks Lena is wrong to hide it.
- **Arc:** her health slowly fails through your teens. Whether she tells you the truth before the end depends
  on how close you are. Her death (around your late teens) is one of the biggest days of your life.

### Your little brother or sister, born when you're 7
- You help name them on the day they're born. They follow you everywhere and copy what you do.
- **Arc:** your choices shape who they become. As teens they can idolise you, resent you, or follow you into
  the same mistakes. As adults, you're each other's last family.

### Your father (never seen in childhood)
- Left when you were a baby. Nobody says why. Mum says you laugh like him.
- **The truth (revealed in the teens):** he didn't simply abandon you. He took the blame for something his
  brother did and went to prison, then was too ashamed to come back. Lena never forgave him for choosing his
  brother over his family. He wrote every year. She never gave you the letters.

### Corin Varell (the man in the green coat), your father's brother
- Sometimes stands across Willow Lane watching your house, then walks away. Gran sends him off angrily once.
- **Arc:** a glimpse in childhood, a conversation in your teens, the key to finding your father. Guilty,
  charming, not to be trusted with money.

## Willow Lane

### Nico Brask, your best friend (same age)
- Two doors down. Never stops talking, dreams big ("one day I'll own a shop and sweets will be free").
  His dad drives deliveries; his mum works at a laundry.
- **Wound:** when you're 9, his dad loses his job and the family struggles more than yours.
- **Arc:** as a kid he steals from Mr Ambrose once, and you see it. As a teen he drifts toward a rough crowd
  from The Rows. You can pull him back, follow him, or let him go. As adults he can be your closest friend,
  your business partner, or a stranger you pass in the street.

### Mrs Pell (Edith Pell), the widow next door, 76
- Sharp tongue, beautiful flowers, very lonely. Watches the lane from her window and knows everything.
- **Secret:** her only son, Daniel, hasn't visited in fifteen years. She tells everyone he's "very busy abroad".
  They fell out over money when her husband died.
- **Arc:** small kindnesses add up. If you're close, she tells you the truth one day, and maybe asks you to
  find Daniel. What she leaves you when she dies depends on you.

### Mr Ambrose (Teo Ambrose), the corner shop, 45
- Jokes with everyone, remembers every child's name, gives credit to families who are short.
- **Secret:** Lena's tab is getting too big, and he can't afford to carry it much longer.
- **Arc:** a big supermarket chain, **DailyVa**, opens nearby when you're 12, and his shop starts to die. As a
  teen you can help him fight back (new ideas, deliveries, a better shop) or watch it close. If it survives,
  it might one day be yours.

## Beyond Willow Lane

### Juno Calder, the rival (same age)
- Lives in the big houses around Kestrel Park. Rich parents, best at everything at Sunhill Primary, and finds
  you irritating from the first day.
- **Secret:** her parents are falling apart, and home is cold and silent. Being perfect is how she copes.
- **Arc:** a rival for decades, a reluctant ally when you find out the truth, possibly more, or your biggest
  competition in business.

### Mr Hale, your teacher at Sunhill Primary
- Notices something in you that nobody else does. Not a guide who tells you what to do, just one adult who
  believes in you, and says so.
- **Arc:** puts you forward for a scholarship at 11 to Urlivaven Central, the best school in the city. Taking
  it means leaving Nico behind.
