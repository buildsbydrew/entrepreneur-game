# Places in Urlivaven

Urlivaven is a modern city in the country of Urlivana. The world opens up as your life does: as a child you
know a few streets; as an adult you know the whole city. Each place is built like Home and Willow Lane today:
walkable, with its own people and their daily routines, joined to its neighbours by walking off an edge (and
later by bus, bike or car).

## How we build them
- **One district at a time**, each as a set of connected places, built from data like today.
- Each place gets its people and routines, everyday activities (sitting, cooking, playing), and lights at night.
- To keep it manageable, most buildings are seen from outside and only story places get full interiors.
