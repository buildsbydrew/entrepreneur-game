# Roadmap: what the game needs to tell this story

The story (in `docs/story/`) asks for things the game can't do yet. This is the list, in the order we'd build
it, so each chapter becomes fully playable before the next.

**Being built now (in its own session):** film mode and the autopilot for the AI life videos; everyday
activities (sit, run, jump, cook, eat, read, play, wave, idle fidgets).

---

## Phase 1: Childhood fully playable (ages 5 to 12)

1. **Big days.** A "story day" written as data: when it happens (age, flags), where you wake, who is where, what
   they're doing, short goals ("Take Mum's note to the shop"), scripted moments (someone walks in, a knock at the
   door), conversations and choices, and what's remembered. The next big day decides when time jumps stop.
2. **Urlivani voices.** Every person's own voice in the made-up language, with emotion, ageing with them; the
   English text stays in the dialogue box.
3. **New places:** Sunhill Primary (classroom, playground, gate), Urlivaven City Hospital (corridor, ward),
   Mrs Pell's house inside, Kestrel Park, the High Street with DailyVa.
4. **The real cast in the game:** Mum (Lena), Gran (Ilsa) and Biscuit the dog, Nico, Mrs Pell, Mr Ambrose, Juno,
   Mr Hale, Corin in the green coat, with looks, routines, everyday activities, and their childhood conversations.
5. **Things you carry:** letters, the carved bird, Mum's note, a key. A small "pockets" list on the People screen.
6. **A new family member:** your little brother or sister is born at 7; you help name them; they grow up and
   remember how you treated them.
7. **Animals:** Biscuit (Gran's dog), and maybe the cat on the car.
8. **Small challenges** for moments like the scholarship exam: a short, simple test of what you've learned
   (skills and earlier choices make it easier).

## Phase 2: The teens (ages 13 to 19)

9. **Getting around the city:** the bus between districts, with a little ride scene; later a bike.
10. **New districts:** Old Harbour, Northgate, The Rows, two schools.
11. **Work and hustles:** part-time jobs, a small business of your own, money coming in, and choices about it.
12. **A phone:** messages from friends and family, which carry drama between big days.
13. **First love:** dates, getting closer, heartbreak; family-friendly.
14. **People who die:** Gran's last days and funeral; what they leave you; how the world remembers them.

## Phase 3: Young adulthood (ages 20 to 29)

15. **Running a shop:** stock, prices, customers, staff, rivals (DailyVa); the old market game, grown up.
16. **Your own home:** renting, moving, furnishing.
17. **Partners, weddings, children:** relationships that become a household; your own children growing up.
18. **Banks, loans and debts:** borrowing, interest, risk, losing things.
19. **Careers** for lives that don't run a shop: nursing (like Mum), office work, a trade.

## Phase 4: Adulthood and old age

20. **Bigger risks:** floods, fires, price shocks, illness; insurance and starting again.
21. **Ageing parents and grandparents:** caring for Mum; your father's last year.
22. **Passing things on:** wills, inheritance, who takes over.
23. **Grandchildren** and becoming the old neighbour.

## Alongside, all the way

- **Shipping:** the web version, then the Android app (APK and Play Store).
- **Sound and music** that grow with each new place.
- **Testing:** automatic checks, plus Drew playing each chapter.
- **The AI life series:** an episode for each chapter as it becomes playable.
