DESCRIPTION
I built a life simulator called Urlivaven, then handed the controls to an AI and told it one thing:
be kind. It was born, took its first steps, walked down Willow Lane alone for the first time, swept
the yard for its first five livs, bought its best friend a fizzy sweet, and kept choosing family,
over and over, for 78 years.

Nobody touched the controls. Every walk, every conversation and every choice was made by the AI,
following its personality. The little robot on screen shows what it picked and why.

Next life: a new personality. Tell me in the comments which one you want to see.
